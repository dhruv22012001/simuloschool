Why the report says "hard" and not "75%"

Every lesson quiz has three tiers. Your child only reaches the next one by showing they understood the last — so the level they reach means more than a raw percentage.

10

Facts stated directly in the lesson. Get 5 or more right and the medium tier unlocks.

5

Medium — apply

Connecting two ideas, or using a rule on a new example. Get 3 or more right to unlock hard.

5

Hard — explain

Reasoning about why something works. Reaching this tier at all is a strong signal.
