Questions, answered

Everything parents ask before starting

Is it suitable for all boards?+
Yes. Lessons are mapped to core concepts across CBSE, ICSE, and major state boards for grades 1–9.
How long is each lesson?+
Every session is designed around 15 minutes — long enough to teach one idea properly, short enough to keep focus.
Can parents customise reports?+
Yes — choose daily, weekly, or both, and pick which subjects you want flagged first.
Is my child's data safe?+
Your child's data is encrypted and never sold or shared with advertisers. It exists only to build your reports.
Can siblings use one account?+
Each child gets their own profile and report, so progress is never mixed up — even on the Family plan.
